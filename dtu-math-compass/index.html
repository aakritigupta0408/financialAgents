Year 1, Semester 1 · Mathematics-I

MA101

Series, single- and multi-variable differential calculus, and matrices. The linear algebra unit especially pays off for the rest of your degree — machine learning, signals, and mechanics all lean on it.

Unit 1

Infinite Series

Sequences and series · comparison, ratio, root and Raabe's tests · alternating series · absolute and conditional convergence · power series.

  • NotesPaul's Online Notes — Series & Sequences — the clearest written walkthrough of every convergence test, with solved examples.
  • VideoDr. Gajendra Purohit — Sequence & Series — exam-style solved problems in Hindi/English, close to DTU question patterns.
  • VideoMIT OCW 18.01SC — Single Variable Calculus — Unit 5 covers infinite series, with recitation problems.
  • BookOpenStax Calculus Vol. 2 (Ch. 5–6) — free, rigorous, with exercises and answers.

Unit 2

Single-Variable Calculus

Rolle's and mean value theorems · Taylor's and Maclaurin's theorems with remainders · indeterminate forms and L'Hôpital's rule · maxima and minima.

  • Video3Blue1Brown — Essence of Calculus — watch this first; builds the intuition every theorem hangs on.
  • VideoProfessor Leonard — Calculus 1 — full university lectures; unhurried and thorough on MVT and Taylor.
  • NotesPaul's Online Notes — Calculus I — quick revision with practice problems per topic.
  • BookOpenStax Calculus Vol. 1 — free replacement for a standard calculus text.

Unit 3

Multivariable Differential Calculus

Partial derivatives · Euler's theorem on homogeneous functions · total derivative and chain rule · Jacobians · Taylor's theorem in two variables · maxima/minima · Lagrange multipliers.

  • VideoKhan Academy — Multivariable Calculus — many of these videos were made by Grant Sanderson (3Blue1Brown); superb visual intuition for partials and Lagrange multipliers.
  • VideoDr. Gajendra Purohit — Partial Differentiation — Euler's theorem and Jacobians drilled the way Indian university papers ask them.
  • VideoMIT OCW 18.02SC — Multivariable Calculus — complete course with problem sets and exams.
  • BookOpenStax Calculus Vol. 3 (Ch. 4) — differentiation of functions of several variables, free.

Unit 4

Matrices & Linear Algebra

Rank and elementary transformations · consistency of linear systems · eigenvalues and eigenvectors · Cayley–Hamilton theorem · diagonalization.

  • Video3Blue1Brown — Essence of Linear Algebra — makes eigenvectors and determinants visual; three hours that change how you see the whole unit.
  • VideoGilbert Strang — MIT 18.06 Linear Algebra — the most famous linear algebra lectures ever recorded.
  • BookBeezer — A First Course in Linear Algebra — free open textbook with hundreds of exercises.
  • BookTreil — Linear Algebra Done Wrong — free; a step deeper, great if the unit clicks and you want more.

Year 1, Semester 2 · Mathematics-II

MA102

Integration in higher dimensions, vector fields, and differential equations — the mathematical toolkit of every core engineering course that follows, from electromagnetics to control systems.

Unit 1

Multiple Integrals

Double and triple integrals · change of order and change of variables · areas and volumes · beta and gamma functions.

  • VideoDr. Gajendra Purohit — Multiple Integrals — change-of-order problems solved exactly like exam questions.
  • NotesPaul's Online Notes — Multiple Integrals — careful region setups, the part students actually lose marks on.
  • VideoMIT OCW 18.02SC — Units 3–4 — double/triple integrals with worked recitations.
  • ToolGeoGebra 3D — plot the region before you integrate; wrong limits are the #1 error.

Unit 2

Vector Calculus

Gradient, divergence, curl · line, surface and volume integrals · Green's theorem · Stokes' theorem · Gauss divergence theorem.

  • NotesMath Insight — Vector Calculus — interactive applets that let you see divergence and curl; nothing else comes close.
  • VideoDr. Gajendra Purohit — Vector Calculus — Green's/Stokes'/Gauss problems in exam format.
  • VideoDr. Trefor Bazett — Vector Calculus — short, visual, English-language topic videos.
  • NotesBetterExplained — Understanding the Gradient — why ∇f points uphill, in plain words.

Unit 3

Ordinary Differential Equations

First-order ODEs (exact, linear, Bernoulli) · higher-order linear equations with constant coefficients · variation of parameters · Euler–Cauchy equations.

  • BookTrench — Elementary Differential Equations — a complete, free ODE textbook with a solutions manual.
  • VideoMIT OCW 18.03SC — Differential Equations — the full course, problem sets included.
  • VideoDr. Gajendra Purohit — Differential Equations — every standard solvable type, with tricks for speed.
  • NotesPaul's Online Notes — Differential Equations — revision-friendly summaries of each method.

Unit 4

Laplace Transforms

Definition and properties · transforms of derivatives and integrals · inverse transforms · convolution theorem · solving ODEs by Laplace transform.

  • VideoDr. Gajendra Purohit — Laplace Transform — the go-to playlist for this unit in Indian universities.
  • VideoKhan Academy — Laplace Transform — builds from the definition up, step by step.
  • VideoSteve Brunton — Engineering Math — why engineers use transforms at all; connects Laplace to the control systems you'll meet later.
  • ToolWolframAlpha — check any transform instantly (laplace transform of t^2 sin(t)).

Year 2 · Mathematics-III and core applied math

Third and fourth semester bring the applied core: PDEs and Fourier methods, complex variables, probability, and numerical computation. Exact course codes and pairings depend on your branch — every branch meets these four areas somewhere in second year.

Sem 3 · Common

PDEs & Fourier Series

Fourier series and half-range expansions · formation of PDEs · separation of variables · wave, heat and Laplace equations · Fourier transforms.

  • Video3Blue1Brown — But what is a Fourier series? — the visual intuition that makes the whole unit click.
  • VideoSteve Brunton — Fourier Analysis & PDEs — beautifully filmed playlists connecting Fourier methods to real engineering.
  • VideoDr. Gajendra Purohit — Fourier Series & PDE — exam-pattern solved problems for every standard equation.
  • NotesPaul's Online Notes — Partial Differential Equations — separation of variables worked slowly and carefully.

Sem 3–4 · Branch-dependent

Complex Analysis

Analytic functions · Cauchy–Riemann equations · complex integration and Cauchy's theorem · Taylor and Laurent series · residues and contour integration.

  • VideoDr. Gajendra Purohit — Complex Analysis — exam-oriented coverage of the standard units.
  • BookBeck et al. — A First Course in Complex Analysis — free open textbook, gentle and complete.
  • VideoNPTEL — Complex Analysis (IIT lectures) — full-length IIT courses matching the Indian syllabus.
  • Notescomplex-analysis.com — free interactive book; drag points and watch mappings deform.

Sem 3–4 · Common

Probability & Statistics

Random variables · binomial, Poisson and normal distributions · expectation and moments · correlation and regression · sampling and hypothesis testing.

  • BookIntroduction to Probability (probabilitycourse.com) — a full free textbook, readable online, with solved problems.
  • VideoMIT OCW 6.041SC — Applied Probability — Tsitsiklis's legendary probability course.
  • ToolSeeing Theory (Brown) — interactive visualizations of every core probability idea.
  • PracticeKhan Academy — Statistics & Probability — graded exercises for distributions and hypothesis testing.

Sem 4 · Common

Numerical Methods

Bisection, Newton–Raphson · interpolation (Newton, Lagrange) · numerical differentiation and integration · Euler and Runge–Kutta for ODEs · solving linear systems.

  • NotesNumerical Methods for STEM (Prof. Kaw) — free full course: videos, notes, and quizzes per method.
  • VideoNPTEL — Numerical Methods (IIT lectures) — matches Indian university syllabi unit for unit.
  • VideoDr. Gajendra Purohit — Numerical Analysis — Newton–Raphson and interpolation in exam format.
  • ToolGoogle Colab — implement each method in Python; coding Runge–Kutta once teaches more than ten solved examples.

Study playbook

How toppers actually use these resources — a sequence, not a pile.

Intuition before procedure

For each new unit, watch the visual treatment first (3Blue1Brown, Math Insight), then learn the solving procedure. Methods stick when you know what they mean.

One written + one video reference

Pick a single book or notes source and a single channel per unit and go deep. Hopping between five playlists is procrastination wearing a productivity costume.

Tutorial sheets are the syllabus

DTU mid-sem and end-sem questions track the tutorial sheets closely. Solve every sheet problem yourself before touching any external practice.

Previous-year papers, timed

Two weeks before exams, switch entirely to past DTU papers under exam timing. Patterns repeat — change of order of integration and Cayley–Hamilton show up almost every year.

Verify with tools, don't lean on them

Use WolframAlpha to check answers after solving by hand, never to skip the hand-solving. The exam hall has no search bar.

Teach one topic per unit

Explain Lagrange multipliers or the convolution theorem to a friend without notes. If you can't, you've recognized it, not learned it.
