01 What it is & why we built it

Kalshi lists a fresh binary every 15 minutes: will BTC be above the strike at the window close? The strike is set near the money at the open, so at decision time the outcome is close to a coin-flip. The house (Kalshi) makes money on the vig (a ~7% fee, maxed at-the-money) and the bid–ask spread — not on forecasting. We built this system to answer one scientific question honestly:

Is there a forecasting edge that survives the vig? Everything here — the data tape, the feature store, the model zoo, the paper-trading arms, and the A/B platform — exists to test that, offline and online, without fooling ourselves.

It is deliberately a pre-guardrail research project: the goal is a correct answer, not a production trading system. The honest current answer is STOP-NULL — no arm beats the market at the open net of vig. That null is the deliverable.

The system runs on a single laptop: a long-lived Python daemon (btc_rl/online.py) captures market data, runs the models, paper-trades several strategy "arms," and settles them on official Kalshi outcomes; a cron chain regenerates JSON snapshots that a static site renders.

05 Features

Two families. Barrier — the analytic distance from price to strike at open+6 min — is the one strong signal. The exogenous microstructure set was built to add orthogonal information; an audit showed most of it is noise or collinear with the barrier.

Target label exact_yes is a clean coin-flip: P(yes) = 0.5027 (n = 6,497), no imbalance or drift. The feature store is PIT-bounded (entry = close − 11 min) and passes a leakage canary (OOS ≈ 0.507, i.e. no future peeking).

The honest FE finding: even with the sign bug fixed, barrier + exo OOS accuracy (0.676) is below barrier alone (0.694). The 7-column microstructure store adds nothing over the 2-column barrier — order-flow, realized drift, and the barrier are the same underlying concept.

The gap is promotion discipline: fm took a live capital slot on a within-noise 3.4 pp offline win (n = 148). The recommended control is the calibrated logit kb3 / market-blend kb2, not the price-MSE DQN.

09 Offline & online evaluation

Kept strictly separate. Offline is prequential/walk-forward on stored predictions; online is the live paper A/B. The decisive experiment is the value-gate backtest — bet only when calibrated p − ask/100 ≥ margin, net of vig.

  • Offline: walk-forward + a sealed TEST_V2 (175/672 rows, never opened); leakage canaries on cron; a leaked Alpha-Vantage "edge" preserved as INVALIDATED. N is honest — only ~195 settled windows, deduped from ~13.7 correlated rows/window.
  • Online: unit = market_window_id, paired per-contract net-EV, moving-block bootstrap. All four live arms straddle 0.
  • Decisive result: value-gate = STOP-NULL — no margin clears the CI above 0.
Known eval gaps (fix list): ad-hoc significance scripts pseudo-replicate (pool minutes not windows → z inflated ~3.6×); the offline econ/value-gate is evaluable on only 1 of 8 arms because decision-time ask_c is logged for one — the other seven should report UNAVAILABLE, not 0.0.

At the open the outcome is a calibrated coin-flip. The in-window accuracy ramp (0.76 → 0.92 as the close nears) is ~74% mechanical random-walk convergence, not forecasting — at the close, market Brier 0.138 ≈ the mechanical 0.137 (zero residual skill). The one real scientific lever — Phase B

The at-open direction problem is at its Bayes ceiling. The only untested question is whether orthogonal, non-price information at the entry survives the vig. Build it as a disciplined ladder, not another arm:

  1. Curated feature store — gated by univariate AUC + dup/collinearity scan, PIT-bounded, on the correct BRTI statistic; add order-flow, cross-venue basis dynamics, funding/liquidation signals — not more price-derived microstructure.
  2. Calibrated model — direct binary log-loss (logit/GBT) + Platt/isotonic calibration; no price-MSE detour.
  3. Ablation ladder — market-blend → barrier-only → +orthogonal, each reported with paired window-level CIs against the market-implied prob at the same decision time.
  4. Sealed test — evaluate finalists once against TEST_V2; a pass must clear the 0.518–0.528 breakeven net of vig with a CI excluding it. Anything less is STOP-NULL — the honest, expected outcome.
The system's real achievement is not an edge — it's an honest, self-auditing measurement apparatus that refused to manufacture one. The correct next deliverable is a clean answer to the orthogonal-information question, not another trading arm.
