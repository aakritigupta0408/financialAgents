Watching the Q-agents learn

The ladder's first rung, batch-trained: eight tabular-Q runs — sparse then shaped reward at the +1/+5/+15/+30 min horizons. Curves are greedy-policy scores on a validation slice (last 15% of training days) the agent never trains on. Dotted line = persistence baseline. Later rungs (t7 linear-Q, t8 distributional DQN, t9 LSTM) batch-train via scripts/train_l2–l4.py; their held-out numbers and the full head-to-head live in experiment review, and the arms' online learning in live predictions.

Hourly retrains — the live gate, across time

The online daemon retrains every arm hourly behind a hold-out no-regression gate, and appends each outcome to metrics_history.jsonl.
