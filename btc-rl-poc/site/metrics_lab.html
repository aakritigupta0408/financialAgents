  4. Brier vs market Brier

Training & loss regimerank 12

What each tier optimizes, and whether the hourly online retrains are actually helping — the hold-out no-regression gate keeps an update only when replay improved hold-out MSE.

Loss functions in use (as documented in code)

  • Tier 1 price arms — shaped prediction reward for the RL update (btc_rl/env.py reward(), replayed with shaped=True in btc_rl/online.py), promoted only through the hourly hold-out MSE no-regression gate ("bad updates revert", btc_rl/online.py).
  • Tier 2 KB logits — online logistic regression, one SGD step per settled call; "log-loss training keeps the output probability naturally calibrated" (btc_rl/agents.py · BinaryLogit).
  • Calibrators — online Platt scaling fit by damped Newton steps on the log-loss with exponentially decayed statistics, so it tracks a moving miscalibration (btc_rl/agents.py · PlattCalibrator).

Leakage & method

Every number on this page is blind-forward: a prediction is written to the log before its outcome exists, and is scored only when the market later settles it. Four rules keep hindsight out of the loop:

  • Prequential test-then-train — each arm is scored on a window first, and only then may train on it; no row is ever scored by a model that has already seen it.
  • Settle-ordered replays — offline replays feed outcomes in settlement order, so no update can peek past its own timestamp.
  • Decision-time-only features — feature vectors are frozen at the minute the call is made; nothing computed after made_ts can enter them.
  • The ≤12-minute envelope — one decision per window, taken inside the same time envelope the live desk trades in, so evaluation and execution see identical information.

The full audit of how these rules were violated once, caught, and remediated is in docs/SEV0_REMEDIATION.md.
