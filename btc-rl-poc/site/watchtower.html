Agents, backend & cost

Who acts, on what trigger, with what authority — and the one thing none of them can do: change live policy. Every promotion, retirement, or policy change terminates with the human. Behind them, the clock face of everything that runs on a timer, and the bill for all of it.

The roster is read from world.json, which mirrors PROGRAM.md §3 and DECISIONS.md; if a card and the repo ever disagree, the repo wins. The activity stream below it is stitched client-side from the same ledgers every other page uses — timestamps in both PT and IST, because the owner is in India trading US markets.
