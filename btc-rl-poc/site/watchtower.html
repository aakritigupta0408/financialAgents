Severity ladder & open incidents

The four-level taxonomy adopted in the OS blueprint (§7) — INCIDENTS.md grades on it. Below the ladder, every incident currently on the books, in whatever state it is in; closed or mitigating, it stays visible.

SEV-0
results cannot be trusted
leakage; eval sees future data; pairing corrupted; wrong settlement; metric changed mid-experiment; logged policy ≠ running policy; unreproducible result
SEV-1
material performance/risk degradation
maxDD breach; EV collapse beyond threshold; stake-cap breach; execution leak beyond bound
SEV-2
degraded subsystem, safe fallback
stale feed within fallback; repeated retrain failures; drift threshold; coverage collapse; missed monitor run
SEV-3
maintenance, no scientific impact
stale dashboard; publisher failure; delayed non-critical report
