Reward design

The spec reward is honest but nearly silent — it fires on ~0.5% of episodes. The shaped variant keeps the same objective while making the gradient audible: misses are measured in band units (u = |error|/band), so every horizon optimizes the same thing, the ramp is continuous at the band edge, and it bottoms out at the spec's own −1.

+1  |pred − actual| ≤ max($5, 0.1·σ_h)
−1  any other outcome (spec)
max(−1, 2 − u)  shaped variant on a miss, u = |error| / band
