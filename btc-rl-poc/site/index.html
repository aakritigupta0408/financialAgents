Reward design

The spec reward is honest but nearly silent — it fires on ~0.5% of episodes. The shaped variant keeps the same objective while making the gradient audible.

+1  int(pred) == int(actual)
−1  any other outcome (spec)
−|error| / 100  shaped variant on a miss
