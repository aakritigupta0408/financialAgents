The $1K Desk

Every paper trader in the protocol on one view — its current bid and status, its last 20 resolved bids with the thesis behind each entry, and the model it uses to decide. Each trader starts with $1,000 of simulated capital and bets one 15-minute Kalshi BTC window at a time at the real ask + fee.

Current bid status: active = open, not yet settled · skipped = declined this window (limit unfilled / gate not met) · resolved = settled. Thesis text is the post-hoc loss review where available, else the entry provenance (which arm was followed and its trailing record). Click a trader to expand its model.
