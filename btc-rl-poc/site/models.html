Model scoreboard

Two planes, judged separately. And one honest rule before reading any of it — good prediction ≠ market information ≠ economic value: a model can top this table and still make no money, because knowing the price is not the same as knowing something the market doesn't, which is not the same as being paid for it after costs. The paper traces that full chain.
