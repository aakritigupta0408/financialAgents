✦ Live experiment · Bitcoin price prediction

BTC 7PM Oracle Treatment ladder — head to head

Four reinforcement-learning treatments, a frozen control, and two naive baselines (persistence, chart-replay) predict Bitcoin's price every 5 minutes at +5/+15/+30 min horizons, scored at integer level against the same market. The first model is the frozen control; treatments must pass the offline gate (scripts/offline_gate.py — MAE + duplicate checks) before deployment; t3/t4/t5 were retired as offline-proven duplicates. Each survivor adds exactly one capability — continuous features, live streams, trend & order book, a Bitcoin-trained LLM, order-flow imbalance — so every metric gap is attributable. All numbers refresh live from the prediction ledger.

How much of the prediction is the last price carried forward (100% = pure chart copy — persistence by construction). Lower = more genuine prediction; rp sits near 0% because it copies the move, not the price.
