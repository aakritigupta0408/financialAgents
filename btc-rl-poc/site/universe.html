zoom level 2 · the clocks

The clocks — what happens when you leave it alone overnight

Nobody sits at this desk at 3am. These cadences do, and each one leaves a file behind to prove it ran.

~1 min Market data + publisher refresh the daemon polls Bitcoin and the Kalshi book, turns raw water into features, and the site republishes what it sees.
15 min A market window settles and is scored every Kalshi 15-minute question resolves UP or DOWN; the predictions, decisions and fills for that window are scored and appended to the ledgers.
10 min The audit chain runs independent evaluators re-derive the math from the raw ledgers, invariants are re-checked, and the agent crew fires on its own sub-cadences.
hourly Evidence-gated retraining candidate weights train, then face a holdout quiz — kept if the quiz improves, reverted if not. Forgetting a bad lesson is normal; keeping one is not allowed.
5 min Watchdog + independent meta-monitor one process watches the machine; a separate one watches the watcher. UNKNOWN is never green.
on fault DETECT→ CONTAIN→ REPAIR→ INDEPENDENT VERIFY→ RESTORED or FAILED_CLOSED FAILED_CLOSED is not a crash — it is the machine protecting the evidence by refusing to act until a human looks.
daily Capture-quality gate recomputed the data harbor re-certifies its own feeds — features built on bad capture never reach the models.

The machine's overnight story is recorded, not narrated — self_heal and incident ledgers hold the receipts.
