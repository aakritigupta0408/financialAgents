The 80/80 scoreboard & bet-policy A/B

Deliverable model kbf: one definitive call per 15-minute window at T-3 minutes — every window called, both classes tracked, no abstention. Class baseline to beat: 80% precision and 80% recall per class. Below it, the live A/B: the bidding model (mandatory one bet per window) vs the selector model — a passive filter that inspects the very same bet the moment it is placed and keeps or skips it (identical bet, price and timing: pure paired selection). The selector's confidence policy retrains once daily at 7 PM PT and is frozen in between; its bankroll epoch starts at policy go-live. Ranking criterion: final bankroll from $1,000 at 2% risked per bet, compounding, fees included.
