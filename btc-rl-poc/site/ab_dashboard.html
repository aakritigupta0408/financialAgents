Evaluation protocol

The standards every number on this page is held to.

  • Point-in-time only: features freeze at the decision instant (live-anchored, strict bucket bounds); settlement uses the candle closing exactly at the target. Convention changes are stamped on rows (anchor_src, settle_v) — nothing is restated.
  • Chronological splits: every offline claim comes from a 70/30 time split; thresholds tune on the past and freeze before the future.
  • Market as the null: each model is scored against the market's own probability on identical calls; phase-stratified so convergence can't masquerade as skill. Anything that fails this check is retired and says so (kbf).
  • Costs in: P&L nets Kalshi's trading fee (⌈7%·price·(1−price)⌉ per contract, ~2¢ at a 65¢ entry) at ask-side fills; bankrolls compound a fixed 2% risk. Kalshi direct charges no commission beyond this; routing via a broker (e.g. Robinhood, $0.01/contract) would cost roughly another 1.5% per $1 at typical entries — not modeled, disclosed here.
  • Uncertainty stated: Wilson 95% lower bounds on every rate, Diebold–Mariano on paired errors, two-proportion z on the A/B.
  • Audit trail: every metric recomputes client-side from the published append-only ledgers (git-SHA-stamped in the repo) — nothing on this page is a server-side claim.
