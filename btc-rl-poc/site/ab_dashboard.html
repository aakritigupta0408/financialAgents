The 80/80 scoreboard & bet-policy A/B

Deliverable models kb2 (market-anchored blend) and kb3 (online learner): a probability on the live Kalshi 15-minute contract every minute, no fixed decision time. Class baseline to beat: 80% precision and 80% recall per class at each model's confidence gate. The earlier fixed-T-3 variant (kbf) is retired as trivial — the market alone matches it at that timestamp. Below it, the live A/B: the bidding model (mandatory one bet per window) vs the selector model — a bet-level EV model that judges the very same bet in the same tick it is placed (whenever the entry strikes — no fixed decision minute, no future information) and keeps it only if its predicted win probability clears both a precision-0.90 target threshold and the break-even price+fee line. It is trained on every settled real bet plus counterfactual bets built from settled per-minute quotes, retrains once daily at 7 PM PT, and is frozen in between; every mandatory bet carries the selector's keep/skip verdict and score, so skips are auditable. Bankroll epoch starts at policy go-live. Ranking criterion: final bankroll from $1,000 at 2% risked per bet, compounding, fees included.

Selector generations — every design, scored like kbf

Three selector designs have gated the same mandatory bet stream. Each is scored on what it kept vs what it declined — a selector only adds value if its kept bets beat its skipped ones. v1 gated on per-minute call confidence; v2 was a bet-level EV model trained on mid-quote counterfactuals (which taught it to bless 76–79¢ favorites that are −EV at the ask — visible below); v3 trains on ask-adjusted prices.
