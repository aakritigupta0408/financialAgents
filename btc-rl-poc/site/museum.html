The Toxic Hour

superstition vs multiplicity
Kid plaque

9 AM looked cursed — the robots kept losing then. But if you search 24 different hours for a cursed one, luck alone makes about two hours look cursed. We found exactly two.

Engineer plaque

Searched 24 hourly buckets for regime-toxic hours. Under the null, ~1.98 false "bad hours" are expected from multiplicity alone; we found 2. Binomial multiplicity test: p = 0.60. Mitigation DROPPED, not deferred (2026-08-28).

The Late Genius

skill without a payout window
Kid plaque

One robot, kbf, made the most accurate predictions of everyone — three minutes before the answer, when it was too late to bet. Being right when it no longer matters pays nothing.

Engineer plaque

Best Brier in the system (0.097) and AUC 0.93 — yet −$0.41 per $1 at real costs. Its skill lives at T−3 min, after market convergence, where no tradable edge remains. Retired.

The Helpful Fixer

calibration chasing drift
Kid plaque

We built a helper to make the robots' confidence more honest. Measured properly, it made every single robot worse — so we filed the paperwork to retire our own invention.

Engineer plaque

Platt layer (M1): prequential log-loss worse than raw for all 9 arms (+0.034..+0.164, kb7 worst). The fitted direction flipped within a day — miscalibration drifts faster than the fit. Retirement proposal filed by its own author (D-m1-future).
