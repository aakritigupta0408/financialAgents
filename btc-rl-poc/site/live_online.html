The kb arm plays this contract: every minute it commits P(close ≥ strike) from t8's predicted distribution, sigma scaled to the time left in the window, with the market's odds logged beside it — the full probability path of each window. All calls settle together at the quarter-hour close (9:00, 9:15, 9:30…); Brier is the squared error of the probability (lower is better) — the head-to-head score of us vs the crowd on identical windows.

Is it learning? live · cumulative

Every 5 minutes, both our model and a "do nothing" baseline predict the same target; whoever lands closer wins that slot. The purple line adds up the dollars of accuracy the model has gained (or given up) vs doing nothing — a rising line means the model is converting learning into value right now; its slope is the live learning payoff. Vertical ticks mark hourly retrains.
