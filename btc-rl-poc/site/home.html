The Desk — five traders, five disciplines TA spec · simulated · not advice

Each starts with $1,000 and pays real asks and fees. 🦊 Dartmon, the Follower places the current best bidder's call (the arm leading its last 10 settled decisions), risking at most 10% of funds. 🐢 Rungmon, the Ladder mirrors every trade uncapped and banks $1k at $11k, then ×10. 🦉 Sagemon, the Disciplined takes any desk entry only at ≥77% confidence. 🐉 Blazemon, the Gambler stakes 33% of his capital on every entry (about 1.6× Kelly at these asks), capped by the LIVE near-touch depth of the book — the aggressive end of the sizing curriculum. 🐿️ Stashmon, the Saver starts with $10k, stakes 25%, and banks 25% of every win as savings that never return to play.
