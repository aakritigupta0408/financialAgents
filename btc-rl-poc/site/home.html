What happens inside?

The whole thesis in one loop — repeated every 15-minute market window, around the clock.

Observe→ Predict→ Estimate uncertainty→ Compare with market→ Decide→ Simulate trade→ Measure outcome→ Learn→ Try again

The machine doesn't assume it's right.

It has to prove every step — and most ideas don't survive the proof.

A model can predict price well and still fail economically. Accuracy is not money. A forecast that arrives after the market has already moved is trivia, not edge. Case: kbf — accurate, too late, retired.
A strategy can make money temporarily and still fail statistically. Small samples flatter. Every result must survive its pre-registered sample size and confidence interval. Case: t_cheap — +13¢/$1 on 36 bets; verdict: keep collecting.
A model can look promising and still be retired. Shadow arms flip sign week to week. Only frozen decision gates speak — and their verdicts are recorded forever. Case: A3-v1.1 — rejected at its gate; the failure taught us more than a win would have.
