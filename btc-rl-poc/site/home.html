Can a learning system call Bitcoin's next 15 minutes?

An always-on experiment: eleven reinforcement-learning arms forecast BTC-USD at +1/+5/+15/+30 minutes while two binary models price the live Kalshi 15-minute contract every minute — scored against the market's own probabilities, with fees in, on public append-only ledgers. The honest headline: at these horizons the market is nearly efficient; the edge this system demonstrates is calibrated probability at disclosed operating points — and it beats the class baseline of 80% precision and 80% recall per class.

The live signal — what the system would do right now paper · research demo

The decision layer, live: kb2's current probability on the open Kalshi window is held against a bet-grade gate — a precision-first threshold that depends on window phase (early calls need near-certainty, late calls less) and backs off during session-open hours where misses cluster. When the gate clears, this panel shows the exact paper order, its economics, and every piece of evidence for and against — the same auditable ledger data as everywhere else. No real orders are placed; this is how the research would be used.

What we disproved — and published anyway

Negative results are results. Every claim below is measured in the repo and stated on the dashboards.

✗ A fixed-time caller is trivial. Our first deliverable (kbf, one call at T-3 minutes, 90.4% accurate) was retired when we measured the market alone at the same instant: 90.9%. Its replacement locks a call the first minute confidence clears a gate — average lock 11+ minutes out.
✗ Minute-scale direction from price alone is a coin flip. The sign of MAE-optimal level forecasts runs 48–54% across all arms — the martingale floor, shown live on the direction chart. Skill exists only against fixed strikes with market anchoring.
✗ Selector v2 subtracted value. Trained on mid-quote counterfactuals but deployed against ask fills, it kept 76–79¢ favorites that were −EV: 43% on kept bets vs 51% on its skips. Diagnosed (mid-vs-ask bias), retired on the public generations table; v3 trains on ask-adjusted prices.
✗ The mandatory bet mandate is −EV. Forced entries plus fees put the always-bet policy under water — measured on 180+ real-priced paper bets; it is the motivation for selection, kept as the honest control.
✓ What survives: calibrated per-minute probabilities above 80/80 at disclosed coverage, decision times that vary with conviction, and an evaluation protocol (point-in-time, chronological, market-as-null, costs-in) that every number on these pages is held to.
